What changed, and why

1

One hero metric, not five equal tiles. Next payroll is the only number on the page with a deadline attached, so it gets the large type, the tint, and the primary action — the rest move to a quiet row underneath.

2

Section labels read as labels, not buttons. "Workspace," "People," "Organization" are now uppercase and muted, sitting above real nav items, so scanning the sidebar doesn't require clicking to learn what's a group versus a page.

3

Team Directory, Employees, and Contractors became one "People" group. The original put the same person in two places under two sections; one roster with a type filter removes the question of where to look.

4

"Quick actions" and "Tasks" merged into one "Needs attention" list. Each row now carries a real severity — blocking payroll, informational, awaiting a decision — instead of four identical gray buttons that don't say which one is urgent.

5

Approve and decline are no longer equal-weight icons side by side. Approve is the filled, primary action; decline is a quieter text button with real space from it — so confirming and rejecting someone's time off aren't one misplaced click apart.

6

"Start an AI chat" moved from a full-width bar into labeled search, plus an empty-state nudge. The permanent entry point is the always-visible "Search or ask AI" field — text, not just an icon, so it doesn't rely on anyone knowing the shortcut. The second entry point only appears when Needs Attention is empty: instead of a blank "all caught up" state, that slot offers an AI suggestion at the one moment it isn't competing with something more urgent.
